# Founding Farmers

2,222 playable farmers. Free mint on October 15.

<figure><img src="https://2398763816-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FnpKFpukS6TEdxHuYNYUd%2Fuploads%2Fgit-blob-1d1b749746e81a605c66dfabc9598ee8018b447d%2Ffounding-farmers-banner.png?alt=media" alt="Founding Farmers in front of the Bloomshire logo"><figcaption><p>Every one of them is a character you play.</p></figcaption></figure>

**2,222** farmers on Robinhood Chain. Each one is a character in Bloomshire. Its look is fixed, and it is exactly how you appear in the game.

{% hint style="success" %}
The mint is on **October 15** on [OpenSea](https://opensea.io/collection/bloomshire-founding-farmers). It is **free** for GTD and FCFS.
{% endhint %}

## The mint

| Phase      | Opens (October 15)      | Price            | Per wallet |
| ---------- | ----------------------- | ---------------- | ---------- |
| **GTD**    | 13:00 UTC               | Free             | 1          |
| **FCFS**   | 15:00 UTC               | Free             | 1          |
| **Public** | 16:00 UTC, for 24 hours | Shown on OpenSea | 5          |

* **Contract:** `0xC53870Cf1381f2848dFfFD2Fd4FfB585bD1b0e9c` on Robinhood Chain.
* You need a little ETH on Robinhood Chain for gas.
* The players who earned a free mint spot in the Founding Farmers story are on the GTD list.

## The reveal

Every farmer shows the same picture until **21:00 UTC on October 15**. Then every farmer turns into itself, with its picture, its moving version and its traits. A farmer minted after that shows at once.

## A fair draw

Before the mint, the order of all 2,222 farmers was shuffled by the hash of **Robinhood Chain block 87,400,000**. The order was fingerprinted before that block existed, so nobody could choose it, us included. After the mint, anyone can check it.

* **Fingerprint (provenance):** `aacc1718944edc207dae5cdfcc23a8900fce3cbff8c2869f917069ed3c9c6ce2`

Rare and Epic farmers wear armour. Legendary and Mythic farmers glow with a faint aura. Each of the twelve Mythics has its own title, like The Bloom Queen and The Sun King. The **Rarity** trait on OpenSea is the one that counts for perks.

## Perks

The perks start with Season 0.

### Every holder

* **Play as your own Founding Farmer.** The moment it reaches your wallet.
* **Season 0 access.** Only holders and the allowlist can play.
* **150 energy** instead of 100, and it **refills twice as fast**.
* **An EXP boost on every skill**, from your rarest farmer. Boosts don't stack.

| Your rarest farmer | EXP boost |
| ------------------ | --------- |
| Common or Uncommon | 5%        |
| Rare               | 7.5%      |
| Epic               | 10%       |
| Legendary          | 12.5%     |
| Mythic             | 15%       |

### The more you hold

| Farmers | Perks                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **2**   | The four Founders' sets: Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter.                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **3**   | A **pet** that follows you, finds forage, carries 5 items and lights up the caves. Pets never attack. The four **Founders' weapon skins**: the Spring Blade, Summer Axe, Autumn Bow and Winter Staff, each with its own attacks. They change how a weapon looks, not what it does. |
| **5**   | **Mounts.**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **7**   | **An exclusive mount skin.** You choose one, once. Crops take **20% less time** to grow. **+5 backpack slots.**                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **10**  | **The VIP area** without the monthly pass. The **Elite Founder badge**. Your name on the **Founders' Hall** wall in town, and a title on your card.                                                                                                                                |

Each count keeps the perks of the counts below it.

### Mythic holders

* **Their own statue** on the avenue to the Founders' Hall, with their farmer's name on it.
* **Their own story** inside the game.
* **An exclusive cosmetic and weapon set.**

### The rules

* **Perks follow the NFT.** Sell or send a farmer and its perks go with it.
* **Your wallets.** The wallet you sign in with counts, and so do the holding wallets you link to your account.
* **24 hours.** A farmer's perks count once you have held it for 24 hours. Playing as it starts at once.
* **One account at a time.** A farmer counts for one account. Once it counts for yours, it stays with yours for 7 days, wherever it moves.
