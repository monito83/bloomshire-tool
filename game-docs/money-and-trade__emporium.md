| Pack                | Bowls | Shards |
| ------------------- | ----- | ------ |
| A Pair of Bowls     | 2     | 150    |
| A Supper for Four   | 4     | 300    |

## Averyn Shards

* **Buy AVS** with ETH or USDG, without leaving the game.
* **Deposit** AVS to get Shards, one for one.

Both use your [Bloomshire Wallet](/money-and-trade/wallet.md). Keep a little ETH in it for fees.
