# Averyn Shards (AVS)

The game's token.

**AVS** is Bloomshire's token on Robinhood Chain. In the game it's called Shards. **1 AVS = 1 Shard.**

{% hint style="warning" %}
Opens **October 1, 12:00 UTC**. The official contract address will only come from the Bloomshire team's announcements. Don't trust any other.
{% endhint %}

* **Buy:** on Uniswap (AVS/USDG), or in the game through the [Emporium](/money-and-trade/emporium.md) or your [wallet](/money-and-trade/wallet.md). The market sets the price.
* **Earn:** by playing. [Lucky drops](/money-and-trade/coins.md#lucky-shards) (up to 30 a day) and 10 for each Founding Farmers chapter.
* **Deposit:** AVS becomes Shards in your purse. The deposited AVS is burned.
* **Withdraw:** turning Shards back into AVS is coming later.
* **Supply:** 50,000,000 AVS at launch. New AVS only comes from withdrawals, with a daily limit for everyone combined.
* **Use:** Moonlight Stew and trading. More to come.

Not financial advice. See [Disclaimers](/disclaimers.md).
