# Mining

Your pickaxe decides what breaks.

Plain rocks give Stone. Seams in the [caves](/the-world/the-caves.md) give ore and gems.

* Every blow on ore costs **2 energy**. Bring food.
* Seams **grow back**, from 20 minutes (copper) to 2 hours (diamond).
* Bram and the General Store buy ore.
