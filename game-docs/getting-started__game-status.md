# Game Status

What's in the game, and what's coming.

Bloomshire is in a **private beta**. Check if your wallet is in on the [home page](https://bloomshire.xyz).

{% hint style="info" %}
The playtest closed on **October 12**. The [Founding Farmers](/token-and-nfts/founding-farmers.md) mint is on **October 15** on OpenSea. The game opens again with **Season 0**, for Founding Farmer holders and the allowlist.
{% endhint %}

## In the game

* Home island, Bloomshire, Whisperwood, Moonmere, Stonepeak and twelve cave levels
* Farming, fishing, mining, woodcutting, foraging, cooking, combat
* 53 quests, including the Founding Farmers story
* Shops, orders, the Bank, the Exchange and the Market Hall
* Chat, friends, leaderboards and face-to-face trading
* Shards for playing: lucky drops and story chapters
* The Averyn Emporium, Averyn Shards (AVS) and the Bloomshire Wallet
* Five languages, and phones

## Updates

Before an update, a line runs across the top of your screen and counts down the minutes. Finish your planting, watering and harvesting before then. When the update goes live, the game reloads by itself.

## Coming later

* Withdrawing Shards as AVS
* Mounts
* Crafting, animal keeping and exploration
* Selling items for crypto
* The Founding Farmers perks, with Season 0
* Land and Farming Points
