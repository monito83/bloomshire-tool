# Moonmere

The lake north of town.

A big lake up the north roads. The best fishing in the game, and where the Founding Farmers story ends.

<figure><img src="https://2398763816-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FnpKFpukS6TEdxHuYNYUd%2Fuploads%2Fgit-blob-3da0caaf933f4a172e40ecd1e5ff1c2a033d0455%2Fmoonmere.png?alt=media" alt="Moonmere"><figcaption><p>The Angler's Hut on the east shore.</p></figcaption></figure>

* **Ghost Catfish:** only here, only at night.
* **Mushrooms** around the shore.
* **The Old Cave:** needs a Bronze pickaxe to get in.
* **Marven,** the lake's mermaid, in the north-west.
