# The Market Hall

Players selling to players.

Talk to **Rowan** and pick **The Marketplace**.

<figure><img src="https://2398763816-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FnpKFpukS6TEdxHuYNYUd%2Fuploads%2Fgit-blob-2172d3d376a777aa56fe9d45ce99caab9ea785ad%2Fmarketplace.png?alt=media" alt="The Marketplace"><figcaption><p>The Marketplace.</p></figcaption></figure>

* **Buy** anything other players have listed.
* **Sell** at your own price, in Gold, Shards or $AVYN.
* Up to **8 listings** at once. Take back what doesn't sell.
* Click any number to type it: how many, and your price.
* The seller pays a **2.5%** fee.
* **Chests** are sold here too: 150,000 Gold.

Selling for ETH and USDG is built, and opens later.
