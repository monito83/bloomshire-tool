# Stonepeak

Mining country, east of town.

Old mining country up the east road, and home to most of the caves.

<figure><img src="https://2398763816-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FnpKFpukS6TEdxHuYNYUd%2Fuploads%2Fgit-blob-f2c15a7350da5d94db30ab99dbb7587f66160946%2Fstonepeak-quarry.png?alt=media" alt="The Quarry"><figcaption><p>The Quarry.</p></figcaption></figure>

* **The Miners' Camp** (north-west): Dunn, the head miner.
* **Five caves:** the Old Working, Topaz Drift, the Green Vein, the Gloom and the Sunken Vault. See [The Caves](/the-world/the-caves.md).
