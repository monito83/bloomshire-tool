# Farming

Dig, sow, water, pick.

<figure><img src="https://2398763816-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FnpKFpukS6TEdxHuYNYUd%2Fuploads%2Fgit-blob-46a24b64171c624c0eed0a5dc7c547b052feede7%2Ffarming.png?alt=media" alt="Crops growing in the island field"><figcaption><p>The carrots at the top are ready.</p></figcaption></figure>

1. **Dig** with the hoe. One swing turns a whole bed.
2. **Sow** one seed per square.
3. **Water** once. Crops over an hour need a second drink near the end. Rain counts.
4. **Pick** with the sickle when ripe.

* Crops grow in real time, even when you're offline.
* Point at a planted square to see what's growing and when it will be ripe. On a phone, stand facing it.
* Sowing a seed and picking a crop cost 1 energy each. Digging and watering are free.
* Some crops **regrow** after picking: one planting gives **5 harvests**, then the plant is gone.
* Planted the wrong thing? The **shovel** (Hazel's Seed & Tool shop, 15,000 Gold) digs up any seed or crop. What you dig up is lost, and the soil stays ready to sow. It never wears out and costs no energy.
* Seeds unlock as your Farming level rises. Herb seeds (Marigold, Sunflower, Aloe) are at the Apothecary, the rest at the Seed & Tool Shop.
* Star Fruit needs Farming level 10.
