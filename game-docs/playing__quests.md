# Quests

The story and the side jobs.

53 quests:

* **The opening** on your island (5)
* **The town** (5)
* **Side quests** from the townsfolk (8)
* **The Founding Farmers**, the main story (18 chapters)
* **Field work** beside the story (17)

<figure><img src="https://2398763816-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FnpKFpukS6TEdxHuYNYUd%2Fuploads%2Fgit-blob-4cef1ce4b4b20347d30bd0a6b198904bc8e339e6%2Fjournal.png?alt=media" alt="The journal"><figcaption><p>The journal.</p></figcaption></figure>

* The **journal** lists your quests and says where to go.
* A **gold marker** shows who or where the quest wants.
* Quest items can't be sold or traded by mistake.
* When a quest wants something you **grew, caught or cooked**, only your own work counts. Bought ones don't.
* A reward that doesn't fit in your hotbar and backpack waits in a chest at home.

## The Founding Farmers

The story of Bloomshire's first settlers. It takes you through Whisperwood, down into the caves of Stonepeak and up to Moonmere.

Some puzzles have answers that are **different for every player**, so you can't copy someone else's.

Every chapter pays **10 Averyn Shards**.

### Field work

Every chapter but the last comes with a crop to grow and harvest. It starts with its chapter, and you can do it any time in that act. The next act waits until it is done.

* Plant in the town fields by the greenhouse, or at home.
* Each task asks for one crop, and only that crop counts. If its seeds are still locked for you, grow other crops first to raise your Farming level.
* Done in order, the field work takes you to **Farming level 10**, in time for the Star Fruit.

{% hint style="success" %}
The **first 1,000 players** to finish the Founding Farmers story get a **free mint spot** and **500 Averyn Shards**.
{% endhint %}
