## Lucky Shards

Now and then, work brings **1 or 2 Shards**. A little shard pops up over you and flies into your purse.

| Doing                                                   | Chance  |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| Harvesting a rare crop (seeds from Farming level 10 up) | 1 in 8  |
| Catching a fish                                         | 1 in 20 |
| Mining ore, felling a tree, foraging                    | 1 in 25 |

Up to **30 lucky Shards a day** (the day starts at 00:00 UTC). Each [Founding Farmers chapter](/playing/quests.md) pays **10 Shards** on top.
