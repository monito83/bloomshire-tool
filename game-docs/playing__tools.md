# Tools

What lasts and what wears out.

* **Never wear out:** hoe, watering can, sickle, shovel, weapons.
* **Wear out:** axe, pickaxe, fishing rod. One use per hit or cast.

* **Pickaxe:** better metal breaks harder ore. See [Mining](/skills/mining.md).
* **Rod:** better metal makes fish easier to catch.
* Buy them from **Bram** (axes, pickaxes) and **Wren** (rods). Cave chests hide better ones.

## Carrying

* You carry **10 things**. There's no backpack.
* Store the rest in your chests at home or at the **Storehouse** in town.
* More chests: 150,000 Gold at the Market Hall.
